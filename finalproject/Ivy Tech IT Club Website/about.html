Who We Are

The IT Club at Ivy Tech South Bend-Elkhart is a student organization focused on technological aspects, video game experiences, and developing social skills with others. We provide a welcoming environment for students to explore IT topics, collaborate on projects, and build connections in the tech and gaming community. Our club is open to all students interested in technology and gaming, regardless of their major or experience level.

What We Offer

Video Games

Access to a variety of video games, whether it be consoles or PCs.

Tech Labs

Tech Labs on a Friday monthly — sometimes with a guest speaker.

Field Trips

Occasional field trips to fun and educational locations.

Snacks & Drinks

Free snacks and drinks during club meetings and events.

Friendships

Opportunities to build friendships and social skills with others.
