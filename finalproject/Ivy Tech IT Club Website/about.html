Who We Are

The Ivy Tech IT Club at Ivy Tech South Bend-Elkhart is a student organization focused on technological aspects, video game experiences, and developing social skills with others. Weekly meetings occur every Friday that include access to video games (consoles and PCs) and Tech Labs where certain technological topics are discussed; guest speakers sometime stop by for these Tech Labs. Field trips are taken on occasion, whether it be to movie theaters, museums, or even events out-of-state.

What We Offer

  • Access to a variety of video games, whether it be consoles or PCs
  • Tech Labs every Friday - sometimes with a guest speaker
  • Occasional Field Trips
  • Free Snacks and Drinks
  • Opportunities to build friendships with and social skills with others
